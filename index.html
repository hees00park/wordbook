A 3,000-entry bank: 2,400 single words and 600 idioms, fixed expressions, and phrasal verbs. Each daily set contains 40 words and 10 expressions, with 30 B2, 15 C1, 3 B1, and 2 C2 entries. Words rotate within each level, with the least recently assigned entries first. A set already in progress keeps its original items.

Mark all 50 daily items as “Know it” or “Review again” to unlock the test. Answer all 50 items, one at a time, in a shuffled order. Score at least 45/50 (90%) to pass. Retakes cover the same 50 entries in a new order. A failed retake does not remove an earlier pass. If two test items have identical meanings and parts of speech, either studied spelling is accepted.

Meanings and example translations are in Korean. The interface and original examples are in English. Click an example word for a local Korean definition; words outside the local dictionary use an online English definition when available. The Korean dictionary link opens a full lookup. Inflected forms are matched to likely base forms, and meaning can vary by context. Content is prepared for this wordbook. Selected B2 word labels were cross-checked by headword and part of speech against the Oxford 3000/5000; individual senses and expression levels remain editorial estimates.

Pronunciation uses an English (United Kingdom) voice. Available voices depend on your browser and device.

Dates reset at midnight in Asia/Seoul. Progress and test answers are stored in this browser. Other devices and browsers do not share these records. Vocabulary ratings from the previous plan are retained; old 200-item plans are archived when today's plan changes.
